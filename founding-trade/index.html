What You're Getting

Every benefit, in plain terms.

20% Lifetime Trade Pricing
The best discount we offer — locked in permanently, regardless of future pricing changes. As our MSRP grows with the brand, your cost basis stays fixed.
Early Inventory Access
First allocation on new stock before it opens to the general market. Your projects don't wait.
Priority Production
Your orders move to the front of the queue during high-demand periods. When inventory is constrained, founding partners are fulfilled first.
Direct Founder Access
A direct line — not a support ticket. Real conversations about your projects, your feedback, and what you need from this collection.
Product Influence
Your feedback shapes what we build next. Founding partners are the first to know about new SKUs, finishes, and product expansions — and the first to be asked.
Locked-In Pricing
Price increases do not apply to founding partners. What you see today is what you pay, always. This clause is written into the founding partner agreement.
