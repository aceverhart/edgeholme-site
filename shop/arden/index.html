  • Entry door pairs — ideal spacing is fixture centered 8–12" from door casing
  • Garage flanking — single fixture on each side of a single-car garage door
  • Garden and landscape walls — single fixture at 6' mounting height
  • Covered porches and breezeways
  • Fence and gate columns

For facades above 12 feet or wide double-door entries, consider the 36" or 48" for better visual proportion. All three sizes use identical mounting hardware and can be mixed across a single property.

The Arden mounts to a standard single-gang outdoor electrical box (new construction or retrofit). All hardware is included. Installation requires a licensed electrician in most jurisdictions.

ROUGH-IN

Single-gang box, centered at desired midpoint height. Wire gauge: 12 or 14 AWG. Ground wire required.

DIMMING

Compatible with most leading-edge (TRIAC) dimmers. Recommended: Lutron Caseta PD-6WCL or Leviton DSL06.

INCLUDED HARDWARE

Mounting plate, gasket, two stainless mounting screws, wire nuts, and rough-in template.
